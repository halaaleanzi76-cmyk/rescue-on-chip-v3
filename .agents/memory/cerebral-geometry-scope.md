name: Cerebral geometry scope
description: Evidence boundary for the vessel graph geometry used by the Rescue-on-Chip vascular twin.
---

Treat current vessel coordinates, lengths, and radii as illustrative engineering fixtures, not measured human anatomy or patient-specific geometry.

**Why:** The source proposal describes a concept-stage ex-vivo microfluidic research platform and supplies no cerebral-vessel imaging or measured geometry; no authorized anatomy dataset was available during the first vascular graph milestone.

**How to apply:** Keep this distinction visible in the UI and reports. Replace fixture values only with traceable, appropriately authorized measurements, and retain source and uncertainty metadata when doing so.
