5 — PYTHON HEMODYNAMIC ENGINE

Implement a separate, modular vascular solver using Python, NumPy, and SciPy.

Begin with a steady-state, reduced-order, laminar vascular network model.

For each eligible idealized cylindrical segment under Newtonian, rigid-wall assumptions:

Hydraulic resistance:

R = 8 × μ × L / (π × r^4)

Segment flow:

Q = (P_in - P_out) / R

Mean velocity:

v = Q / (π × r^2)

Wall shear stress magnitude:

τ = 4 × μ × |Q| / (π × r^3)

Use these equations only within their stated physical assumptions.

Compute pressures and flows for a connected network using conservation of mass:

Sum of signed flows at every internal junction = 0.

Solve the network pressures using an appropriate sparse linear system.

Use SI units internally:

Pressure: Pa
Flow: m³/s
Viscosity: Pa·s
Length and radius: m
Velocity: m/s
Shear stress: Pa

Allow scientifically appropriate display conversions, including mmHg, µm, mm/s, and µL/min.

Validate all conversions.

Reject invalid or nonphysical parameter values.

Do not treat this reduced-order model as equivalent to complete human blood physiology.
